# Business Insights

## 1. Executive Summary

The e-commerce business generated ₹622,700 in revenue from 200 orders
and 50 customers. The business sold 499 units and generated ₹294,010
in gross profit, resulting in a gross profit margin of 47.22%.

The average order value was ₹3,113.50, while every customer in the
dataset placed more than one order. This indicates strong repeat
purchasing behavior within the available dataset.

However, the analysis also identifies significant opportunities to
improve marketing efficiency and reduce product returns.

## 3. Sales & Profitability Insights

### Strong Overall Profitability

The business generated ₹294,010 in gross profit from ₹622,700
of revenue, producing a gross profit margin of 47.22%.

This indicates a strong overall product-level margin before other
operating expenses such as marketing, logistics and administration.

### High Average Order Value

The average order value was ₹3,113.50 across 200 orders.

This provides an opportunity to increase customer value further through
cross-selling, product bundles and premium product recommendations.

---

## 4. Customer Insights

### Strong Repeat Purchasing

All 50 customers in the dataset were repeat customers.

This means the dataset contains:

- 50 active customers
- 50 repeat customers
- 0 one-time customers

The average number of orders per customer was:

**200 orders / 50 customers = 4 orders per customer**

This indicates strong repeat purchasing behavior within the dataset.

> Note: The 100% repeat-customer rate should not be interpreted as
> a general real-world retention rate because the analysis does not
> use a defined customer cohort or retention time window.

### Business Implication

The business should focus on:

- Customer loyalty programs
- Personalized recommendations
- Cross-selling
- Repeat-purchase incentives
- Customer lifetime value optimization

**₹108,941.70**

### Profit Leaders

**₹56,644**

Fashion followed closely with:

**₹55,941.70**

### Highest Margin Category

Books achieved the highest profit margin:

**56.21%**

Fashion also showed a strong margin of:

**51.35%**

### Lowest Margin Category

Electronics generated the highest revenue but had the lowest margin
among the categories:

**39.10%**

### Business Implication

Electronics should be reviewed for:

- Supplier pricing
- Product-level margins
- Discounting strategy
- Procurement costs

Meanwhile, high-margin categories such as Books and Fashion may
provide opportunities for increased promotion and assortment expansion.

---

## 6. Marketing Insights

Marketing performance shows a major difference between customer
acquisition volume and campaign profitability.

### Best Campaign

**New Customer Welcome**

- Budget: ₹50,000
- Customers acquired: 42
- Orders generated: 65
- Revenue generated: ₹52,000
- ROI: 4%

This was the only campaign with positive ROI.

### Weak Campaign Performance

9 out of 10 campaigns generated negative ROI under the available
revenue-versus-budget calculation.

The lowest ROI was generated by:

**Fitness Challenge: -42.67%**

The Mega Weekend Sale also showed weak efficiency:

**ROI: -41.00%**

Despite generating the highest campaign revenue of ₹118,000.

### Business Implication

Marketing decisions should not be based only on:

- Customers acquired
- Orders generated
- Revenue generated

Campaign profitability should also be considered.

Future campaigns should prioritize channels and campaign types that
demonstrate positive or improving ROI.

---

## 7. Returns & Operations Insights

The business recorded:

- 30 returns
- ₹72,815.60 in refunds
- 15% return rate

Returns therefore represent a significant operational and financial
area for improvement.

### Highest Refund Product

The product with the highest individual refund exposure was:

**Non-Stick Cookware Set — ₹11,225.10**

Other high-refund products included:

- Smart Watch Pro — ₹8,077.10
- Slim Fit Jeans — ₹6,295.00
- Remote Control Car — ₹6,197.00
- Hydrating Face Cream — ₹5,081.10

### Return Reasons

Each return reason represented exactly 20% of total returns.

However, the financial impact varied.

The largest refund exposure came from:

**Product not as expected — ₹23,288**

This was followed by:

**Wrong product received — ₹14,888**

### Business Implication

The business should consider:

- Improving product descriptions and images
- Strengthening order-picking verification
- Improving packaging standards
- Monitoring product quality
- Investigating high-refund products individually

Reducing returns could improve both customer satisfaction and
profitability.

## 9. Key Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Electronics Profitability

Electronics has the highest revenue but only a 39.10% gross margin.

Review procurement costs, discount levels and product-level margins.

### 2. Scale High-Margin Categories

Books and Fashion demonstrate attractive margins.

Consider expanding assortment, promotions and cross-selling within
these categories.

### 3. Improve Marketing ROI

Only one campaign achieved positive ROI.

Future campaigns should use ROI and customer value as primary
decision-making metrics rather than acquisition volume alone.

### 4. Reduce Product Returns

The 15% return rate represents a significant opportunity.

Prioritize products and categories with high refund values and
investigate the causes of returns.

### 5. Leverage Repeat Customers

All 50 customers were repeat customers in the dataset.

Use loyalty programs, personalized recommendations and cross-selling
to increase customer lifetime value.

### 6. Increase Average Order Value

The current average order value is ₹3,113.50.

Bundles, complementary products and premium recommendations could
help increase order value.

---

## 10. Overall Business Conclusion

The e-commerce business demonstrates strong revenue generation,
profitability and repeat purchasing behavior.

The biggest opportunities are not basic sales growth alone, but
improving the efficiency of existing operations.

The most important priorities are:

1. Improve marketing ROI
2. Reduce product returns
3. Improve Electronics profitability
4. Scale high-margin categories
5. Increase customer lifetime value
6. Increase average order value

These insights provide the foundation for the Power BI executive
dashboard and management reporting layer of this project.
